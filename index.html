01 / Research

Can we trust what a machine made?

How generated media and agent outputs can be marked, how those marks can be attacked, and how the genuine can be told from the forged.

  1. Adversarial analysis of generative watermarking

    Exposing weaknesses of semantic-aware and diffusion-noise watermarks.

  2. Robust watermarking design

    Semantic-latent embedding that resists removal and regeneration.

  3. Attribution and forensics for AI-generated content

    Attribution watermarks for LLM-agent trajectories, and what forensics can recover.

  4. Proactive deepfake forensics

    Layered audio-visual watermarks for detection and tamper localization.

  5. Reliability and privacy of LLM agents

    Certifying that updated agents keep earlier skills; privacy of retries.

  6. Robust learning under deficient supervision

    LLM-augmented graph learning with noisy or incomplete labels.
