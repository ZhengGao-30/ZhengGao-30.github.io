01 / Research

Can we trust what a machine made?

I work on the security and provenance of AI-generated content: how generated media and agent outputs can be marked, how those marks can be attacked, and how the genuine can be told from the forged.

01 Mark

How do we sign what a generative model produces?

Watermarks that live inside diffusion models and inside the trajectories of LLM agents.

Semantic-latent embedding · Attribution · Provenance

02 Break

How do those signatures fail under attack?

Semantic manipulation and trajectory deflection show where today’s watermarks give way.

Removal attacks · Regeneration · Semantic rewriting

03 Detect

How do we tell the genuine from the forged?

Layered audio-visual marks detect deepfakes and locate the tampered segments.

Deepfake detection · Tamper localization · Forensics

Active research threads

  1. Adversarial analysis of generative watermarking

    exposing structural vulnerabilities of semantic-aware and diffusion-noise watermarks through LLM-guided semantic manipulation and stochastic trajectory deflection.

  2. Robust watermarking design

    semantic-latent embedding schemes for diffusion models that balance imperceptibility with resistance to removal and regeneration attacks.

  3. Attribution and forensics for AI-generated content

    two-channel attribution watermarks for LLM-agent decision trajectories, tamper-localizing watermarks for coding-agent trajectories, visual provenance across generated and code-rendered media, and information-theoretic characterization of what watermark forensics can and cannot recover.

  4. Proactive deepfake forensics

    layered audio-visual watermarking that supports both detection and segment-level tampering localization.

  5. Reliability and privacy of LLM agents

    certifying that updated agents retain earlier capabilities, and privacy accounting for validation-retry wrappers.

  6. Robust learning under deficient supervision

    large-language-model–augmented graph learning under noisy or incomplete supervision.
